Research

SOLE AUTHOR · MANUSCRIPT, 2026

Echo-Labelling-Free Room Geometry Estimation via Image-Source Trilateration and Statistical Verification

Yitong Luo

To recover a room's walls from echo times of arrival, you normally have to know which echo at each microphone came from which wall. Sorting that out is exponential in the number of microphones. My method, ISTV, never labels echoes. It trilaterates every compatible triple of picks into an image-source hypothesis, verifies it on the remaining receivers, and accepts it only if the agreement is statistically significant against a Poisson clutter model. I prove that this bounds the expected number of false walls by one user-set parameter α.

16–22%
from the Cramér–Rao bound, N ≥ 40 receivers, SNR ≥ 16 dB
135 mics
processed jointly on the FLAIR real-world dataset in 0.33 s
1.6° · 25 mm
error on 3 of 4 recovered walls vs. laser scan
CO-AUTHOR · MANUSCRIPT, 2026

Deep Learning Estimation of the Number and Locations of Surfaces for Underwater ISAC

Yiwen Lu, Mikhail Saleev, Yitong Luo, Allison Sproul, Rene Alzina, Toros Arikan · University of Notre Dame

For 6G-style integrated sensing and communications underwater, a system first needs to know how many reflecting boundaries surround it and where they are, from a single snapshot at low SNR. We extended the U-COTANS method, a multi-stage U-Net that reads images of common-tangent curves, from two boundaries at short range to up to four boundaries at long range in shallow water. The changes included SAGE time-delay estimation, combinatorial echo matching and 400° images that handle the wrap-around between quadrants.

≈3 dB
SNR gain over least squares at N = 1
200k
training images per SNR level, 9 levels from 13 to 21 dB
